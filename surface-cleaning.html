Surface Cleaning

Every Surface. Restored.

Utah's climate is hard on outdoor surfaces. Winter road salt, spring runoff, summer BBQ grease, pollen, and algae all take a toll on your patio, driveway, and walkways. Over time, these surfaces lose their original look and can become slippery safety hazards.

Our hot-water pressure washing system penetrates deep into concrete, pavers, brick, and stone — dissolving grime, killing algae and mold at the root, and restoring your surfaces to like-new condition. We use specialized surface-cleaning attachments that clean evenly without leaving streaks or missed lines.
