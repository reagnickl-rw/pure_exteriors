Common Questions

Window Washing FAQ

For Utah homeowners, we recommend at least twice per year — once in spring to remove winter grime and mineral deposits, and once in fall before the cold season. Homes near construction sites, heavy tree coverage, or with hard water may benefit from more frequent cleanings.
Our DI/RO (deionized/reverse osmosis) system removes 99% of dissolved minerals from the water before it touches your glass. When standard tap water dries, it leaves behind calcium, magnesium, and other minerals as spots. Our pure water leaves nothing behind — just spotless glass. No squeegee needed, no streaks possible.
Our standard service focuses on exterior windows, which are exposed to the elements and accumulate the most buildup. Interior window cleaning can be added for an additional cost — just mention it when you request your quote and we'll include it in the estimate.
Most residential jobs are completed in 1–3 hours depending on the size of the home and number of windows. A standard 3-bedroom home typically takes about 90 minutes. We'll give you a time estimate when we quote your job so you can plan accordingly.
Rain won't leave spots on your windows — it's the minerals in tap water that cause spots, not rain. Rainwater is naturally pure and will simply rinse your windows clean. If we clean your windows and a storm rolls through the same day, your windows will still look great.
