Utah Sun & Your Windows

Cracked Sills Are a Utah-Wide Problem

Even Layton's newer homes aren't immune. Once a window is 8–10 years old, walk the exterior and look closely at the rubber sills around the glass — there's a good chance you'll start to see hairline cracks, gaps, or rubber that's gone brittle and pulled back from the frame.

That's the Utah sun. Direct, intense, year-round sunlight dries out window seal rubber faster than almost anywhere else in the country. Once a seal cracks, the argon gas between the panes of your insulated glass unit slowly leaks out — and along with it, the energy efficiency you paid for. A double-pane window with a failed seal isn't quite single-pane, but it's close.

Repair is a separate trade — we don't do window replacement or reseal work. But here's the part many homeowners don't realize: regular professional cleaning meaningfully extends window longevity. Hard-water mineral deposits, sap, dust, and grit accelerate seal failure by physically abrading the rubber every time the wind kicks up. Keeping the glass and frame clean removes those abrasives and gives the seal a longer working life.

When we're on-site, we'll flag any sills that look like they need attention — so you can plan replacement before they fail completely.
